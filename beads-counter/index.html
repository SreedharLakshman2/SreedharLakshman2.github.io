Beads Counter Support

iPhone and iPad · iOS 16 or later · Lifestyle

This page is support for Beads Counter, a digital finger / tasbih / mala tally. There is no account. Your counts and history stay on this device.

Getting started

  • Tap the large silver button to add one bead. Sound, glow, and spoken count are on by default.
  • The small button resets the session and saves it in History.
  • Open Themes to switch Ocean Night, Lotus Garden, Aurora Sky, and more.
  • Set a target such as 108. The app can stop, vibrate, and celebrate when you reach it.

Fullscreen

Fullscreen hides extra chrome. Tap Exit full screen at the top, or open Settings and turn Fullscreen off.

Ads and tracking

Banner and occasional full-screen ads from Google AdMob keep the app free. After launch, iOS may ask for tracking permission. Allow or Ask App Not to Track. Ads still appear either way.
