Privacy Policy for Beads Counter

Last updated: September 3, 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreedhar.beadsCounter

Beads Counter is a digital tally for prayer beads, meditation, and everyday counting. Your counts stay on your device. The app is free and shows third-party ads.

Information stored on your device

The app stores locally (UserDefaults):

  • Current count, selected theme, target, and session start time
  • History of completed sessions
  • Today and lifetime totals
  • Sound, speech, vibration, night mode, fullscreen, and keep-awake preferences

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies. There is no account or login.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner ads and occasional interstitial ads. Google may collect advertising identifiers and device data according to Google’s policies, to show and measure ads.

On iOS 14.5+ we ask for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) after the launch splash, before ads start. You can refuse. Ads still appear; they may be less personalized.

Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information we do not collect ourselves

  • No name, email, or precise location for our servers
  • We do not sell your count history
  • We do not use your practice data for our own analytics products

Children’s privacy

The app is for a general audience including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are provided by Google; we do not target children.

Data retention and deletion

Delete the app to remove local counts, history, and settings.
