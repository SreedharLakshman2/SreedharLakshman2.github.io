Privacy Policy for Coin Toss - Sreeo

Last updated: August 21, 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreeo.coinToss

Coin Toss - Sreeo is a coin-flip utility. Toss history, the selected country coin, and settings stay on your device. The app is free and shows third-party ads.

Who publishes this app

Sai Laksha Technologies publishes Coin Toss - Sreeo. Developer: Sreedhar Lakshmanan.

Information stored on your device

The app stores locally (UserDefaults):

  • Selected country coin and light/dark appearance
  • Sound and haptics preferences
  • Toss counts (heads vs tails) and toss history for the on-device Stats and History tabs

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies. There is no account or login.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner ads on every screen and occasional full-screen ads after a toss.

On iOS 14.5 and later we may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission so ads can be more relevant. You can decline. The app still works, and ads can still appear.

Google may collect a device identifier and related advertising data according to Google’s policies, to show and measure ads. That data is not linked to an account we hold.

Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google’s advertising page: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Information we do not collect ourselves

  • No email, phone number, or account
  • No contacts, photos, precise location, or files
  • No Sai Laksha Technologies analytics or crash reporter
  • We do not sell your data or upload toss results to our servers

Children’s privacy

Coin Toss - Sreeo is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

App Store privacy label

Device ID and advertising data may be used by Google AdMob for third-party advertising and analytics, and Device ID may be used to track you across apps if you allow tracking. Product interaction, coarse location, crash data, and performance data may also be used as described in App Store Connect. None of this is linked to an identity we hold.

Data retention and deletion

All app preferences remain on your device until you delete the app or use Reset toss stats in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page.
