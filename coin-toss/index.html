Coin Toss - Sreeo Support

iPhone and iPad · iOS 16 or later · Entertainment

This page is for questions and support for the Coin Toss - Sreeo app. There is no account. Toss history stays on your device. The app is a decision coin flip. It is not gambling, betting, or wagering, and it does not award prizes.

Ask a question or request support

Email: sreedharlakshmanan4@gmail.com

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. We read every message.

Getting started

  • Open the Toss tab and tap Toss to flip the 3D coin.
  • Tap the country chip to pick another coin. Search by country or currency.
  • Open History for past flips, Stats for heads and tails counts, and Settings for sound, haptics, and appearance.

Share and reset

After a toss you can share a result card. In Settings, Reset toss stats clears heads, tails, and history on this device. Deleting the app also removes that data.

Ads

A small Google AdMob banner can appear on every screen, and a full-screen ad may show every few tosses so the app can stay free. After launch, iOS may ask for tracking permission so ads can be more relevant. Allow or decline; the app still works either way.

Privacy

Toss history, the selected coin, and settings stay on this device. See the Privacy Policy.
