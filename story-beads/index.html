Story Beads Support

iPhone and iPad · iOS 16 or later · Education

This page is for questions and support for the Story Beads app. There is no account. Story progress stays on your device. A small banner on the home screen helps keep the app free. Stories and games have no ads.

Ask a question or request support

Email: sreedharlakshmanan4@gmail.com

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. We read every message.

Getting started

  • Open the app and pick English, Hindi, Tamil, or Telugu.
  • Tap a story, listen, then tap Let’s play.
  • Choose the right picture to light a bead. Finish the mala to unlock a sticker.

Stories

Four stories to start: Baby Hanuman and the Sun, Little Krishna, The Kind Prince, and Moon Folk Tale. They are written for ages 5–8: brave, kind, and never scary.

Playground

  • Bead Garden — plant flowers in order from 1 to 10.
  • Diya Match — find the matching pairs.

Bedtime, voice, and stickers

Turn on Bedtime for a night sky and a slower, softer live voice. Turn Voice off if you only want to read. Tap the greeting to type a first name. Finish a story to add its sticker to Your stickers. Tap Today’s star once a day to keep a streak.

Ads

One Google AdMob banner appears on the home screen only. Stories and games stay ad-free. Ads are tagged child-directed, G-rated, and non-personalized. The app does not ask to track you.

Reset progress

Language, name, stickers, and the daily star stay on this device. Delete the app to erase them. Reinstalling starts fresh. This cannot be undone.

Privacy

Story progress is stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
