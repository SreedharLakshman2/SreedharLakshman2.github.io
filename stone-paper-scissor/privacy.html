Privacy Policy for Duo Hands

Last updated: August 23, 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreedhar.StonePaperScissorsFun

Duo Hands is a local multiplayer and vs-AI hand-game app (Stone Paper Scissor and Hand Cricket). Scores, streaks, and settings stay on your device. The app is free and shows third-party ads.

Who publishes this app

Sai Laksha Technologies publishes Duo Hands. Developer: Sreedhar Lakshmanan.

Information stored on your device

The app stores locally (UserDefaults):

  • Player display name
  • Sound, music, haptics, and light/dark appearance
  • Win, loss, draw, streak, and games-played statistics

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies. There is no account or login.

Nearby multiplayer

When you play nearby, the app uses the local network and Bluetooth to find another iPhone running the same mode. Your chosen player name and each move or cricket number are sent only to the connected device. We do not relay that traffic through our servers.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner ads on play screens and occasional full-screen ads after a match.

On iOS 14.5 and later we may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission so ads can be more relevant. You can decline. The app still works, and ads can still appear.

Google may collect a device identifier and related advertising data according to Google’s policies, to show and measure ads. That data is not linked to an account we hold.

Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google’s advertising page: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Authorized sellers are listed at https://sreedharlakshman2.github.io/app-ads.txt.

Analytics

The app includes Firebase Analytics. Google may record anonymous app events to understand stability and usage. This is not linked to your name or email by Sai Laksha Technologies.

Information we do not collect ourselves

  • No email, phone number, or account
  • No contacts, photos, precise location, or files
  • We do not sell your data or upload match results to our servers

Children’s privacy

Duo Hands is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

App Store privacy label

Device ID and advertising data may be used by Google AdMob for third-party advertising, and Device ID may be used to track you across apps if you allow tracking. Product interaction may be used for analytics. None of this is linked to an identity we hold.

Data retention and deletion

All app preferences remain on your device until you delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page.
