Stone Paper Scissor Support

iPhone and iPad · iOS 16 or later · Games · Bundle ID com.sreedhar.StonePaperScissorsFun

This page is for questions and support for the Stone Paper Scissor app. There is no account. Scores and statistics stay on your device. Play vs ROBO, or with a friend on a nearby iPhone.

Getting started

  • Tap PLAY NOW for Stone Paper Scissor vs ROBO or Nearby Multiplayer.
  • Tap Hand Cricket to bat and bowl with numbers 1 to 6, vs ROBO or Play Nearby.
  • Set a player name in Settings so friends see it nearby.

Nearby multiplayer

  • Both phones must open the same game. RPS: Play Now → Nearby Multiplayer. Cricket: Hand Cricket → Play Nearby.
  • Do not mix modes. A cricket search will not find an RPS phone.
  • Keep both screens unlocked. Allow Local Network and Bluetooth when iOS asks.
  • There is no code to type. When the other name appears, tap Start Match.

Ads

A Google AdMob banner can appear on play screens, and a full-screen ad may show after a match so the app can stay free. After launch, iOS may ask for tracking permission so ads can be more relevant. Allow or decline; the app still works either way.

Authorized ad sellers for Sai Laksha Technologies apps are listed at app-ads.txt on this site root. That file is what Google AdMob uses to verify this app.

Privacy

Statistics and your player name stay on this device. Nearby moves are sent only to the connected iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
