Pulse Raid Support

iPhone · iOS 17 or later · Games

This page is support for Pulse Raid, a sci-fi gesture combat game. Pick Alien Hive or Robot Legion, throw Claw, Shield, or Beam, then unlock morphs and chassis with XP. Progress stays on your device. There is no account.

Need help?

Open this support page from Settings in the app, or visit the Privacy Policy.

Getting started

  • Choose Hive or Legion, then play first to 3 pulses.
  • Claw rips Shield. Shield deflects Beam. Beam melts Claw.
  • Bank XP after each raid and unlock skins in the Armory.
  • Settings has appearance (system, light, dark), sound, haptics, and Rate Pulse Raid.

Ads

A Google AdMob banner can appear at the bottom of the app. A full-screen ad may show after you finish a raid so the app can stay free. iOS may ask for tracking permission so ads can be more relevant. Allow or decline; the app still works either way.

Authorized sellers for Sai Laksha Technologies apps are listed at https://sreedharlakshman2.github.io/app-ads.txt.

Privacy

Raid stats, XP, and equipped skins stay on this device. See the Privacy Policy.
