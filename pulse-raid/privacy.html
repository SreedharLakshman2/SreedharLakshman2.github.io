Privacy Policy for Pulse Raid

Last updated: August 23, 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreedhar.PulseRaid

Pulse Raid is a single-player gesture combat game. Raid stats, XP, unlocked skins, and settings stay on your device. The app is free and shows third-party ads.

Who publishes this app

Sai Laksha Technologies publishes Pulse Raid. Developer: Sreedhar Lakshmanan.

Information stored on your device

The app stores locally (UserDefaults):

  • XP, wins, losses, raids played, and equipped morphs or chassis
  • Preferred faction, difficulty, sound, haptics, and light/dark appearance
  • Whether onboarding has been shown

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies. There is no account or login.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show a banner at the bottom of the app and a full-screen ad after you complete a raid.

On iOS 14.5 and later we may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission so ads can be more relevant. You can decline. The app still works, and ads can still appear.

Google may collect a device identifier and related advertising data according to Google’s policies, to show and measure ads. That data is not linked to an account we hold.

Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google’s advertising page: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Authorized sellers are listed at https://sreedharlakshman2.github.io/app-ads.txt.

Information we do not collect ourselves

  • No email, phone number, or account
  • No contacts, photos, precise location, or files
  • No Sai Laksha Technologies analytics or crash reporter
  • We do not sell your data or upload raid results to our servers

Children’s privacy

Pulse Raid is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

App Store privacy label

Device ID and advertising data may be used by Google AdMob for third-party advertising, and Device ID may be used to track you across apps if you allow tracking. None of this is linked to an identity we hold.

Data retention and deletion

All app preferences remain on your device until you delete the app or use Reset Progress in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page.
