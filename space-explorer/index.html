Space Explorer Academy Support

iPhone · iOS 17 or later · Kids educational app

This page is for questions and support for the Space Explorer Academy app (on-device name: Space Explorer). There is no account. Progress stays on your iPhone. The app is paid, with no ads and no tracking.

Getting started

  • Open the app, swipe through onboarding, and choose an explorer name.
  • Mercury is unlocked first. Later planets unlock in order as you explore.
  • Home is Mission Control. Explore, Objects, Academy, and Collection are the other tabs.

Planets, objects, and missions

On Explore, tap a planet to learn about it. After the eight planets, dwarf planets and moons become available. Objects has more space bodies. Missions include Moon and Mars flights. Astronaut Academy lessons unlock challenges after you finish the first four modules.

Sound, narration, and haptics

Turn these on or off in Settings. Narration uses the iPhone’s built-in voice and works without an internet connection.

Notifications

The Notifications setting is a preference only. Version 1.0 does not schedule notifications or send push messages.

Ratings and outgoing links

Kids apps must ask a grown-up before leaving the app. Rate, Privacy Policy, and Support use a short math check. Apple also limits how often the in-app rating dialog can appear.

Reset progress

Settings → Reset Progress clears XP, streaks, planets, quizzes, missions, and badges on this iPhone. It cannot be undone.

Family Sharing

If Family Sharing is enabled on the App Store listing, family members can use the download you purchased. Progress is stored separately on each device.

Privacy

Learning progress is stored only on your device. We do not collect personal data. See the Privacy Policy.
