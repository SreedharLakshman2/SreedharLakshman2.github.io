Little Scientist Support

iPhone and iPad · iOS 17 or later · Education · Ages 6–12

This page is for questions and support for the Little Scientist app. There is no account. Experiment progress, XP, and badges stay on your device. The app is free and shows Google ads that are not personalized. The app does not track users across other apps or websites.

Getting started

  • Enter a scientist name, then tap Start Experimenting.
  • On Home, open Today’s experiment and tap Start Experiment.
  • Each lab follows the same loop: predict, experiment, observe, learn why, take a short quiz, and earn XP.
  • Use Explore to pick any of the seven labs. My Lab keeps experiments you have started. Progress shows XP, badges, and discoveries.

Seven labs

  • Volcano Explosion — mix baking soda and vinegar and erupt
  • Solar System — spin planets and see day and night
  • Gravity Lab — drop objects on Earth, the Moon, Mars, and Jupiter
  • Electricity Lab — close the circuit and light the bulb
  • Human Body Lab — tap organs and see how they work
  • Light & Colors — mix red, green, and blue, then split a rainbow
  • Weather Lab — change temperature and moisture to make rain

Settings and Parent Zone

In Profile, open Settings for sounds, haptics, and Listen & Learn (on-device read-aloud). Parent Zone is behind a simple grown-up math question so children cannot open it by accident. It shows a learning snapshot: labs completed, time, quiz accuracy, streak, badges, and discoveries.

Reset

Deleting the app removes the scientist name, progress, XP, badges, and settings from this device. There is no cloud backup.

Ads

Little Scientist is free. A banner sits above the tab bar. A full-screen ad may appear after a lab is finished and when a lab is opened from Explore. Ads are served by Google AdMob, tagged as child-directed, and not personalized. The app does not ask for tracking permission.

Privacy

Progress and settings stay on this device. See the Privacy Policy.
