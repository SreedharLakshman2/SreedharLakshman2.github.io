Privacy Policy for Little Scientist

Last updated: 21 August 2026 · Bundle ID com.littlescientist.app

Little Scientist is a children’s science lab. We designed it so that a child’s learning stays on their device. The app is free. It shows Google ads that are child-directed and not personalized. The app does not track users across other apps or websites, and it does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

Who publishes this app

Sai Laksha Technologies publishes Little Scientist. Developer: Sreedhar Lakshmanan.

Information stored on your device

The app stores locally (on-device storage such as UserDefaults and SwiftData):

  • Scientist display name
  • Experiment progress, XP, badges, discoveries, and streak
  • Sound, haptics, and Listen & Learn preferences

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies. There is no account or login.

Voice

Listen & Learn uses Apple’s on-device speech synthesis to read text aloud. Speech is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies.

Ads

Google AdMob shows a banner above the tab bar and occasional full-screen ads after a lab is finished and when a lab is opened from Explore. We tag ad requests as child-directed and request non-personalized ads. Google’s advertising systems may process a device advertising identifier and technical information to serve and measure those ads. We do not use that identifier to track children across apps or websites, and the app does not show Apple’s tracking permission prompt.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information we do not collect

  • No email, phone number, or account from Sai Laksha Technologies
  • No contacts, photos, precise location, or files
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites by Sai Laksha Technologies
  • No Sai Laksha Technologies analytics or crash reporter
  • We do not sell personal information or upload progress to our servers

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for children ages 6 to 12 with a parent or guardian. Ads are treated as child-directed under COPPA-related Google settings. Parent Zone is behind a simple grown-up math question so children cannot change those settings by accident. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own use.

App Store privacy label

Little Scientist does not track users. Device identifiers may be used by Google AdMob for third-party advertising. They are not linked to your identity by Sai Laksha Technologies. Apple APIs used on-device include UserDefaults (app settings) and file timestamps as declared in the app’s privacy manifest.

Data retention and deletion

All app data remains on your device until you delete the app. There is no cloud backup.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page.
