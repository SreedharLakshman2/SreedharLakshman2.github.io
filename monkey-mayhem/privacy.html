Privacy Policy for Monkey Mayhem

Last updated: 18 September 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreeo.monkeymayhem

Monkey Mayhem is a kids jungle jumper for iPhone and iPad. Children can play without creating an account, seeing ads, or sending personal information to us.

Who publishes this app

Sai Laksha Technologies publishes Monkey Mayhem under the Sreeo Studio brand.

Information stored on your device

The app stores locally (UserDefaults): bananas, stars, outfits, stickers, missions, and settings such as music and Easy Mode. A local flag is used only to time Apple’s in-app rating prompt.

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies. There is no account or login.

Information we do not collect

  • No email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or files
  • No advertising identifiers and no tracking
  • No analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or third-party ads
  • No in-app purchases

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Links that open outside the app are behind a parental math check. Parents can erase on-device progress with Settings → Reset Progress.

App Store privacy label

We declare Data Not Collected. Local progress does not leave the device.
