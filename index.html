Count Mantras Support

iPhone and iPad · iOS 16 or later

This page is for questions and support for the Count Mantras app. There is no account. Your japa counts stay on your device.

Getting started

  • Choose a language, then tap Continue.
  • On the Japa tab, tap the lotus to count one bead. A mala is 108.
  • Open Mantras for Gayatri, Hanuman Chalisa, and other stotras.
  • Progress shows today’s malas, lifetime japa, and your streak.

Lock-screen and volume counting

On the Japa tab, turn on Lock-screen counting. Then:

  • Press volume up or volume down to count a bead (works in the app and on the lock screen).
  • Or use Play / Next on the lock screen or in Control Center.

Turn the toggle off if you do not want background audio.

Ads and tracking

Banner ads from Google AdMob keep the app free. After launch you may see Apple’s tracking prompt. You can Allow or Ask App Not to Track. Ads still appear either way.

Languages, sound, and haptics

Change language, counting sound, haptics, and keep-screen-awake in Settings.

Privacy

Practice data is stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
