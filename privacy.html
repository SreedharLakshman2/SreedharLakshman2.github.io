Privacy Policy for Count Mantras

Last updated: August 20, 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreedhar.countMantras · Apple ID 6448906516

Count Mantras is a japa (mantra counting) app. Your practice data stays on your device. The app is free and shows third-party ads.

Information stored on your device

The app stores locally (UserDefaults):

  • Selected mantra, current mala progress, malas completed, lifetime japas, daily streak, and recent session history
  • Language, sound, haptics, keep-awake, and lock-screen preferences

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies. There is no account or login.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner ads (and interstitial ads when configured). Google may collect advertising identifiers and device data according to Google’s policies, to show and measure ads.

On iOS 14.5+ we ask for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before ads start. You can refuse. Ads still appear; they may be less personalized.

Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information we do not collect ourselves

  • No name, email, or precise location for our servers
  • We do not sell your japa history
  • We do not use your mantra practice for our own analytics products

Audio and lock screen

If lock-screen counting is on, the app may play a soft drone so iOS keeps a Now Playing session. That audio stays on the device.

Children’s privacy

The app is for a general audience including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are provided by Google; we do not target children.

Data retention and deletion

Delete the app to remove local japa data.
