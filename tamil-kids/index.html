Getting started

  • On first launch, type the child’s name and choose girl or boy.
  • On Home, tap Start on today’s mission. The letter train shows உயிர் letters.
  • Each letter follows Learn → Trace → Write → Find → stars → World Map.
  • Learn covers உயிர், மெய், உயிர்மெய், words, numbers, and writing.
  • Play has short games. Rewards shows XP, badges, and the map.

Parent area

Parent is behind a parental gate. Each visit, and each time you open another app in the App Store, a grown-up solves a new addition problem. There is no saved PIN and the gate cannot be turned off. After that you can see learning time, change the child’s name and look, turn sound on or off, reset progress, or open our other apps.

Voice

Listen uses the iPhone or iPad’s built-in speech (Tamil when available). Turn the device volume up. Sound can be toggled in Parent.

Reset

Reset child progress in Parent, or delete the app, to remove XP and lessons from this device. There is no cloud backup.

Privacy

Progress stays on this device. See the Privacy Policy.
