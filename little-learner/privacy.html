Privacy Policy for Little Learner - Sreeo

Last updated: 24 August 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreeo.littleLearner

Little Learner - Sreeo is a children’s app for letters, numbers, writing, and math. A child’s progress stays on their device. There is no account. Version 1.0 does not show ads and does not ask for tracking permission.

Who publishes this app

Sai Laksha Technologies publishes Little Learner - Sreeo. Developer: Sreedhar Lakshmanan.

Information stored on your device

The app stores locally:

  • Child display name and girl/boy look (for the Home greeting and avatar)
  • XP, stars, streak, badges, and world unlocks
  • Lesson progress for alphabet, numbers, writing, math, and tables
  • Sound preference and parent PIN
  • Weekly learning-time totals

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies. There is no login.

Voice

Listen uses Apple’s on-device speech synthesis. Speech is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies.

Information we do not collect

  • No email, phone number, or account
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No Sai Laksha Technologies analytics or crash reporter
  • We do not sell personal information or upload progress to our servers

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for children ages 4 to 8 with a parent or guardian. The Parent area is behind a PIN so a child cannot change grown-up settings by accident. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own use.

App Store privacy label

Little Learner - Sreeo does not collect data that leaves the device. Sai Laksha Technologies does not track users. On-device storage is used only for progress and settings.

Data retention and deletion

All app data remains on the device until you delete the app or use Reset child progress in Parent. There is no cloud backup.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page.
