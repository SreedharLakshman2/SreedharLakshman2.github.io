Privacy Policy for Gita

Last updated: 10 September 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreeo.gita

Gita is a Bhagavad Gita reading app. Reading language, bookmarks, and progress stay on your device. There is no account and no cloud library in this version. The iOS app is free and may show third-party ads.

Information stored on your device

  • Selected language and appearance
  • Bookmarks, favorites, and continue-reading position
  • A local reading streak and verse history
  • A morning verse reminder, if you turn notifications on

This data is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies.

Audio

Recitation uses on-device speech. If you create a Personal Voice on iPhone, or add your own recordings, those stay on this device. Soft background music may play from a file bundled in the app. Audio is not uploaded.

Notifications

If you turn on verse reminders, a local notification is scheduled on this device. The reminder is not sent through our servers.

Advertising

The iOS app may show Google AdMob banner ads above the tab bar. Apple may ask for permission to track you across apps and websites so ads can be more relevant. You can choose Ask App Not to Track. If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use a device identifier. Ads are not shown on full-screen reading, audio, About, or Privacy pages.

Google’s advertising privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Authorized ad sellers for this developer website: app-ads.txt.

Information we do not collect

  • No login or profile name
  • No location tracking for our servers
  • We do not sell reading history

Children’s privacy

The app is for a general audience, including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are provided by Google; we do not target children.

Data deletion

Delete the app to remove local reading data.
