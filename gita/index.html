Gita Support

iPhone and iPad · iOS 16 or later

This page is for questions and support for the Gita app. There is no account. Reading language, bookmarks, and progress stay on your device.

Ask a question or request support

Email: sreedharlakshmanan4@gmail.com

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. We read every message.

Getting started

  • Choose a language, then begin reading.
  • Open a verse to read Sanskrit, English, Tamil, Hindi, and other meanings.
  • Tap Listen to hear recitation in the language you selected.
  • Save verses from the reader. Listen on a saved verse uses that verse’s language.

Ads and tracking

Banner ads from Google AdMob appear above the tab bar and keep the app free. After launch you may see Apple’s tracking prompt. You can Allow or Ask App Not to Track. Ads still appear either way. Authorized sellers are listed at app-ads.txt.

Audio

Recitation uses a voice on your device. Soft temple music can play in the background and can be turned off from Home or Profile.

Privacy

Reading data is stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
